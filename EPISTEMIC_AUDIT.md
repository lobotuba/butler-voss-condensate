# Epistemic audit — what each result actually is

A construction-vs-discovery audit of the whitepaper's Section 8 results, plus the
Phase-3 empirical core. The purpose is to calibrate the claims: to separate what
the model *measured* (an output that could have come out otherwise) from what it
*re-derived* (known physics), *demonstrated as possible* (existence), *put in and
read back* (construction), or *walked back* (honest negative). This validates the
project's **honesty**, not its contact with experiment — that gap is stated at the end.

## Taxonomy

Each result carries a primary tag (and sometimes a secondary), on the axis of *how
much could it have come out differently*:

- **O — Output.** A non-obvious quantitative result the model was **not built to give**;
  it could have falsified the thesis, and several did. Highest evidential weight.
- **X — Existence.** A mechanism shown to be **possible** in-model; qualitative "it
  happens," not a forced number.
- **K — Known physics / theorem.** Reproduces or instantiates a standard result
  (bulk-boundary correspondence, Deser bootstrap, anomaly inflow, the GR radiation
  law). Real, consistency-level, **not novel**.
- **C — Construction.** An ingredient **put in and measured back out**; largely
  definitional given the setup.
- **N — Honest negative / retraction.** Failed, walked back, regulator-limited, or
  explicitly left open.

## The distribution, in one paragraph

Almost nothing in the suite is pure **construction** — only the Standard-Model-input
sector (§8.41, §8.43, §8.48), and each flags its own input status in-text. The gravity
audit arc (§8.25–§8.37) is dominated by **honest negatives**: the model reached for
Einstein (γ = 1) and every single route returned the scalar answer γ = 0. The genuine
**outputs** are the evidential core, and they are credible *precisely because the same
apparatus produces the negatives* — an instrument that refutes its operator's hopes is
one to trust when it confirms them. A large block is **known-physics re-derivation**
(the effective theory behaves like standard field theory / GR), which is consistency,
not discovery. **External** validation is essentially absent — one untested
Lorentz-violation prediction, and GR-formula reproductions that are consistency rather
than new predictions.

## Coherence flag — RESOLVED (WP-35)

The audit surfaced an apparent internal tension: §8.12's result headline asserted the
induced graviton is "Einstein in the infrared," while §8.32–§8.37 conclude γ = 0 at every
scale. WP-35 resolves it, in §8.12 itself (new reconciliation paragraph + tightened

distinct objects were being read under one "γ": (a) whether the spin-2 graviton **exists
and is healthy** — a property of the induced propagator, *measured* and affirmative; and
(b) whether a static mass **couples to that graviton's spatial polarizations** — the PPN
ratio γ = Ψ/Φ, the thing an experiment weighs. Weinberg's theorem joins them only
conditionally: *if* the mass couples to the massless spin-2, the coupling is forced to be

modulus-squared is derived. Net: no result changes, the claim is re-scoped from "from the medium
alone" to "from the medium's ℏ, given the wave." §8.6, §8.6 result, and the Table-5 QM row edited;
the "guidance not emergent / measurement a postulate" boundary was already honest and stands.
